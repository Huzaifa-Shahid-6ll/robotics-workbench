# Code of conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Discuss ideas and evidence without attacking people. Make room for different experience levels, explain assumptions, and respect consent and privacy around hardware and personal data.

Harassment, discriminatory remarks, threats, unwanted personal attention, and publishing someone else's private information are unacceptable in project spaces. Maintainers may remove harmful content or restrict participation. To report a concern, use GitHub's reporting controls or contact a maintainer privately. Include enough context to review the concern without reposting private material.
